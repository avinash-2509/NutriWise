### Three things they said

1. “Whenever I buy packaged food like biscuits, chips, or juice, I usually don't understand all the nutritional information written on the back. There are so many things like calories, sugar, carbohydrates, saturated fat, and sodium. I know that too much sugar or fat is not good, but I don't know how much is actually too much. Also, some packets show values per 100 grams, while others show them per serving, which makes comparing products confusing.”
2. “Most of the time, I check the price, expiry date, and brand. Sometimes I look at the protein or sugar content, especially if I'm trying to eat healthier. But I don't usually read the full ingredients list because it's long and contains words I don't understand. If two products are similar, I generally choose based on taste or price rather than comparing their nutritional values.”
3. “I think it would be really helpful if I could just take a photo of the food packet and the app could explain everything in simple language. For example, if I'm trying to reduce sugar, it should tell me whether that product fits my goal and explain why. It would be even better if it could compare two products and show which one is more suitable for me. I wouldn't want to manually enter all the information every time.”
