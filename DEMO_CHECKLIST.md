# Three-Minute Demo Checklist

Record a real running session; do not use this checklist as evidence that a video exists.

## Before recording

- [ ] Backend and frontend are running.
- [ ] `data.csv` contains at least 20 genuine, validated observations.
- [ ] The physical packet and its row can be explained.
- [ ] At least one clear nutrition-label photograph is available for OCR.
- [ ] No private information is visible in the browser, terminal, or photographs.

## Suggested three-minute flow

1. **0:00–0:25 — Problem and person:** state the real location, date, target person, and exact difficulty.
2. **0:25–0:50 — Goal:** choose the goal that reflects the interview and show browser persistence.
3. **0:50–1:30 — Scan and confirm:** upload a genuine label, point out an uncertain/missing field, correct it, and confirm.
4. **1:30–2:05 — Analysis:** change serving quantity and show that missing values remain “not declared.”
5. **2:05–2:35 — Recommendation:** explain one positive factor, one concern, the applied rule, and the disclaimer.
6. **2:35–2:55 — Real dataset:** search the CSV library and compare compatible products.
7. **2:55–3:00 — Limitation:** name one honest failure mode, such as OCR or an incompatible comparison basis.

## After recording

- [ ] Watch the full recording and verify text is readable.
- [ ] Add the actual video link to `README.md`.
- [ ] Add genuine screenshots to `evidence/screenshots/` and reference them from the README.
